About Us

Made for people who love to create.

Oserp Atelier is a stationery and paper goods brand made for creative people and communities. We believe that the things we use every day can be more than just practical.

A notebook can hold an idea. A sheet of paper can become the start of something new. Even a small piece of stationery can make the creative process a little more enjoyable.

Our mission is to create stationery that gives people room to explore, create, and express themselves in their own way. We want Oserp Atelier to be more than a brand. We want it to be a place where creative people can find things they enjoy using, share what they make, and feel part of a community that values creativity.

Everything we make starts with that idea, create something you can make your own.
